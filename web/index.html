How it works

Ask, route, pay, prove.

One call from your agent. Axiom does the finding, the paying and the proving, and hands back an answer you can cite.

1 · ASK

Plain language in

Send any question as text. No provider to pick, no schema to learn.

2 · ROUTE

Top-ranked specialist

Axiom picks the specialist ranked highest for that kind of question on the Telegraph protocol, by independent ranking.

3 · PAY

Per call, from 0.01 USD

The specialist is paid for this one answer. No subscription, no key to manage on your side.

4 · PROVE

Cited answer, on-chain receipt

You get the answer, its source and a receipt hash that resolves on a public node for anyone to verify.
